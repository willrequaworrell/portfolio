FinderlyFix is an AI home-repair assistant that carries diagnosis and project context across photos, text, sound, and live video. It helps people diagnose home-repair problems and decide whether to fix them or hire a professional. Will has been a part-time founding software engineer there since September 2025. He owns major parts of its multimodal AI conversation, memory, and project-synchronization experience; built text and live-video assistance with shared context; and contributes product design, usability direction, manual QA, and response-performance work. The product uses React Native, Expo, TypeScript, LiveKit, and the Vercel AI SDK.
