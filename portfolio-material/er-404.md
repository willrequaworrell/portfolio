ER-404 is a primarily solo, publicly accessible interactive browser drum machine by Will. Its central engineering challenge is keeping real-time audio, signal processing, and interface state synchronized while a dense control surface remains legible. Will built its responsive sequencing, effects, and live feedback with React, TypeScript, Tone.js, Vite, and Motion.
